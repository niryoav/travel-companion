# Product vision

Travel Companion is an offline-first travel assistant that reduces stress and
maximizes enjoyment while travelling. It should quietly surface what matters,
help people act with confidence, and remain useful when connectivity does not.

## Experience principles

- **Home is a one-screen briefing.** It presents the most useful context and next
  actions without becoming a dashboard of everything.
- **Today always means today.** It is the dependable chronological view of the
  current travel day.
- **Home adapts to the trip phase.** Before, during, and after a trip, its briefing
  changes automatically to match the traveler’s needs.
- **Offline-first.** Essential information remains available without a network.
- **No connectivity dead ends.** Missing connectivity must not produce an empty
  screen; show useful cached or local information instead.
- **Freshness is visible.** Cached information is shown with when it was last
  updated when freshness matters.
- **Notifications are actionable.** Every notification should help the traveler
  make a decision or complete an action.
- **AI is supportive, not central.** The core product remains understandable and
  useful without AI.
- **Search is global.** One search will cover the whole trip rather than separate
  feature-specific searches.
- **Trips are shared; actions have owners.** Shared trip context coexists with
  personal responsibility and private information.
- **Mobile comes first.** Primary actions are readable, reachable, and dependable
  on a phone.
- **The experience is simple, premium, and calm.** Clear hierarchy and restraint
  take priority over density and decoration.

## Primary navigation

The approved primary destinations are:

1. Home
2. Today
3. Trip
4. Documents
5. More

These destinations are stable product concepts. Their contents will evolve
incrementally as real travel functionality is introduced.

## Product test

Features should reduce friction, enrich the experience, or preserve meaningful
memories. If a feature does not make the journey more useful, enjoyable, or
memorable, it should not be prioritized.
