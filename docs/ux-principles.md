# UX principles

The experience should feel calm, dependable, and useful on a phone.

## Principles
- Prioritize the next action over secondary information.
- Keep navigation simple and predictable.
- Use clear hierarchy, readable text, and large touch targets.
- Support poor or no connectivity for essential content.
- Avoid unnecessary screens, settings, and visual clutter.
- Design for loading, empty, error, and offline states from the start.

## Working approach
Before adding a screen, define its purpose, primary action, content hierarchy, and edge states.
