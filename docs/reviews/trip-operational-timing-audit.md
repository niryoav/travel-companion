# Trip operational timing audit

Reviewed: 30 July 2026

This audit consolidates operational facts supplied and verified by the product
owner for the Oceania Marina 2026 trip. It separates source facts, agreed
planning estimates, and information that still requires confirmation. The full
table is an internal review artifact and is not shown in the traveler UI.

## Status and source conventions

- **Confirmed** — explicitly stated by a booking, ticket, provider, official
  cruise material, or accepted trip data.
- **Estimated** — a planning time derived from confirmed facts. It remains
  editable from Trip and does not become confirmed merely because it has a
  numeric value.
- **TBC** — missing or awaiting confirmation. It appears in Home or Today only
  when it changes a traveler action.
- **Source: operational audit** — verified fact supplied by the product owner
  in the 30 July 2026 operational audit.
- **Source: agreed trip data** — planning intention approved by the product
  owner rather than a provider-confirmed time.

## Actionable UI content selected

- Home receives only the current context, next action, one critical timing,
  actionable weather implication, and at most one preparation alert.
- Today receives the chronological operational milestones, meeting points,
  TBC items that require action, relevant documents, actionable weather, and
  scenario-specific take/dress/provided guidance.
- Trip remains the sole place to edit estimates, TBC operational fields,
  excursion times, tender times, and All Aboard.
- When a regular port has no stored All Aboard value, the traveler-facing
  planning fallback is ship departure minus 30 minutes. The numeric time is
  always labelled as an estimate and TBC; it is not an Oceania-confirmed rule.
  A stored confirmed All Aboard value replaces it. No fallback is generated
  for embarkation, disembarkation, or a port without ship departure.
- Last tender is independent of All Aboard. It remains TBC until explicitly
  entered and is never inferred from ship departure or the planning fallback.
- Húsavík includes breakfast actions to prepare lunch boxes and fill drink
  bottles because the two-excursion sequence is tight and no included lunch is
  confirmed.
