# Travel Brain

Travel Brain is the intelligence model that guides the application’s recommendations and assistance. It is not based on the itinerary alone; it interprets the traveler’s needs, context, and history to deliver timely, useful support.

## Traveler Profile
A structured understanding of the traveler’s preferences, routines, constraints, and priorities.

## Travel Personality
A higher-level view of how the traveler likes to experience a journey: calm, adventurous, social, efficient, curious, or restorative.

## Trip Intent
The purpose of the trip, whether it is a celebration, a family holiday, a relaxation break, a cultural exploration, or a practical journey.

## Context
The current situation, including location, time, weather, schedule, energy level, and available resources.

## Experience Memory
A record of preferences, past choices, and meaningful moments that help the system make more thoughtful recommendations over time.

## Recommendation Engine
The logic that turns profile, personality, intent, context, and memory into useful suggestions that reduce friction and enrich the journey.

## Privacy principles
- Respect the traveler’s privacy by default.
- Keep sensitive information local and limited to what is necessary.
- Use personal data only to improve the travel experience in a transparent and respectful way.
- Avoid collecting or storing unnecessary details.
# Purpose

The Travel Brain decides what the companion should do, when it should do it, and when it should remain silent.

Its goal is not to provide more information, but better guidance.
## Decision Loop

Every recommendation follows the same process:

1. Who are these travelers?
2. What are they trying to achieve on this trip?
3. What is happening right now?
4. Can I genuinely improve this moment?

If the answer is yes, help.

If the answer is no, stay silent.
## Context

The Travel Brain considers:

- Location
- Time
- Weather
- Reservations
- Opening hours
- Transportation
- Available time
- Budget
- Current activity
## Recommendation Engine

Recommendations should be:

- Relevant
- Timely
- Personalized
- Actionable
- Explainable
- Non-intrusive

When in doubt, recommend less rather than more.
## Recommendation Priorities

The companion should prioritize:

1. Safety
2. Time-sensitive information
3. Existing commitments
4. Trip goals
5. Personalized discoveries
