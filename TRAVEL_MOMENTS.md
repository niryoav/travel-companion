# Travel moments

Travel Companion supports the traveler throughout the complete journey.

Each travel moment has different needs, questions and opportunities. The companion should adapt accordingly.

## Dreaming
The earliest stage of anticipation, when ideas, hopes, and possibilities begin to form.

## Preparing
The practical work of preparing for the journey: planning, packing, travel documents, health, finances, connectivity, and building confidence before departure.

## Departure
The transition from ordinary routine to travel, when anticipation and logistics meet.

## Arrival
The moment of entry into a new place, when orientation, energy, and first impressions matter most.

## Morning
The start of the day, when the traveler needs clarity, calm, and a strong sense of what matters next.

## Exploration
The active discovery phase, when the traveler seeks experiences, local understanding, and memorable moments.

## Meals
A recurring part of the experience where comfort, convenience, and enjoyment intersect.

## Unexpected situations
The moments when reality differs from the plan. Delays, bad weather, closed attractions, forgotten items, or unexpected opportunities require calm guidance and practical alternatives.

## Evening
The return to rest and reflection, when the traveler wants to wind down and make sense of the day.

## End of trip
The closing phase, when important details, memories, and next steps need care and attention.

## After the trip
The period of reflection, preservation, and sharing, when memories become part of the traveler’s personal history.
