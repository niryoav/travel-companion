# Product Vision

Travel Companion exists to help travelers create extraordinary journeys and lasting memories.

Its purpose is to help travelers experience more, worry less, and make every trip more meaningful.

By understanding the travelers, the purpose of their trip, and the context of the moment, the companion quietly removes friction while enriching the experience when it matters most.

The best companion is often the quietest one: present when it adds value, invisible when it doesn't.

Every feature should serve at least one of three purposes:

- Reduce friction
- Enrich experiences
- Preserve memories

## Product Principle: The Camera Test

Every feature should pass the Camera Test:

> If this feature did not exist, would the journey become less enjoyable or less memorable?

If the answer is no, it should not be prioritized.
